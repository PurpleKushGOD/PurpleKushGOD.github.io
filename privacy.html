Privacy Policy – Relaks Studio & Shorty

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Relaks Studio and Shorty are private tools used only by their author to manage his own YouTube channels.

What data is accessed

With the channel owner's consent (Google OAuth), the tools use the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and read basic statistics (views, likes, comments) of the owner's own videos. Scopes: youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.

How data is used and stored

The OAuth token and video statistics are stored only on the author's own computer and home server. They are used solely to publish videos and show statistics to the author. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Other users

The tools are not available to the public and do not collect personal data of any other person.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Through the About page of the channels on YouTube, or the support e-mail shown on the Google sign-in screen.
