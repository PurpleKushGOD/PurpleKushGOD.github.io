Relaks Studio & Shorty

Relaks Studio and Shorty are private desktop tools used by a single creator to publish videos to his own YouTube channels (LucidSoulDreams and VideoClipDrop). Relaks Studio creates long relaxation videos; Shorty cuts the creator's own long videos into Shorts.

The tools use the YouTube Data API only to upload videos, set thumbnails and read view statistics of the creator's own channels. They are not offered to the public and do not access anyone else's data.
